An editorial product language

Media and news surfaces with their own token namespace (--tj-*), a serif reading face (XCharter, loaded by the page entry) and a quiet two-voice typographic system: Charter for reading, the grotesque for chrome. Rubric heads, feed cards, the reading column, the community composer and the site chrome itself — for media sites, blogs and corporate journals.

The sub-kit is fully standalone (FR-17): its components and tokens have zero runtime dependencies on the bank family. This page loads the bank token sheet only because the shared site chrome around the demos consumes it — the editorial scopes below would run without it. Names, titles and counts are synthetic.

Rubric grid

The landing head of a rubric: tj-rubric-header composes a clipped cover, a squircle mark overlapping its border by half and the rubric’s own h1 (the component never renders a heading). Article typography

tj-prose is the reading column: a typography container, not a styled paragraph. The lead and body flow in Charter; the grotesque interrupts twice — section subheads and the pull-quote. In-paragraph links are tj-link (color never moves; hover only reveals the underline), and tj-cta is the quiet navigation plate — a 44×44 hit box with a 30px visible pill. Vertical spacing between subheads and quotes is consumer CSS, composed from the token scale.

Composer & feed

The community pair. tj-composer is the fake-input card — a real button, not a textarea: the editor stays on your side, and the kit’s whole contract is the open-compose event (click / Enter / Space). tj-post-card is the transparent community cell: clamped to two lines with the full title mirrored into the anchor’s tooltip, riding a consumer-owned white sheet.

Site chrome

tj-header is the sticky bar: pill navigation from an items array, the current section marked with aria-current only, a built-in theme button (the stateless auto → light → dark cycle — try it) and a “Write” CTA. It compresses 70 → 56 on any scroll. tj-rail is the section rail: icon tiles, the same quiet current marking, and below 1200px it collapses into a burger that opens a focus-trapped drawer.
